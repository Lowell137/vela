# Ideas

A backlog of ideas that are **not** in the [roadmap](roadmap.md): they have
not been decided yet, and nothing here is a commitment. The roadmap stays the
source of truth for the work Vela does; an idea moves there (and gets a
design document) when it is picked up, or becomes an issue that describes
the behavior first (see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).

They are written down so they are not lost, and so they can be looked at
again later.

## Settings

- **Updates, after the page**: show the release notes ("what's new") once an
  update is installed; roll back to the previous package from the page (the
  updater keeps the last three); check automatically (daily, weekly, never)
  and pause updates; install and restart the session in one step.
- **Storage**: a page like Windows' Storage: the space used by each folder,
  the caches and temporary files that can be cleared, and a "Storage Sense"
  that cleans on its own.
- **VPN**: connections in Settings > Network (WireGuard, OpenVPN), with a
  quick switch in the quick settings.
- **Languages per app**: a language for each app, and the spell-check
  dictionary that goes with it, like Windows' language preferences.
- **Restore defaults**: a button that puts a page back to its defaults.

## Accessibility

- **Screen reader**: an accessible reading of the shell's windows (Orca-like):
  the focused widget, the menus, the notifications.
- **Magnifier**: a zoom around the pointer, and a full-screen mode.

## Files

- **Archives**: opening .zip, .tar.* and .7z from Files (and creating them),
  with the password prompt.

## The shell and the apps

- **Light and dark on a schedule**: switching the mode at sunrise and sunset,
  or following the brightness of the wallpaper.
- **Flatpak** (and other sources) in the installed apps page: installing and
  updating apps from Flatpak next to the system ones.

## This fork: themes and styles

- **The greeter and the lock screen follow the theme**: the login screen
  with the user's wallpaper and colors, and the lock screen with them too.
- **More WindowGlass variants**: a brighter and a darker one, and a variant
  tinted with the accent color.
